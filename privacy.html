Privacy

Chem Exam 2 Prep · last updated November 7, 2026

Chem Exam 2 Prep is a personal study site for a general chemistry course. Most of it works without signing in and keeps your progress only in your own browser.

Signing in with Google

The Ask Claude tab uses Google sign-in. The site receives your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. It does not get your password, contacts, files or any other Google data.

What is stored

  • On your device: your study progress (in local storage) and, while the tab is open, your sign-in token and Ask Claude conversation (in session storage). Use Reset progress or Sign out to clear them.
  • On the site's server: if you request access, your email address, name, an optional note you write and the time of the request. If you're approved, your email address and name stay on the list until the owner removes you.
  • Your questions and Claude's answers are not saved on the site's server.

Who processes your data

  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Cloudflare runs the small server that checks your sign-in and keeps the access list.
  • Anthropic receives your questions to generate Claude's answers, under its commercial terms.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site itself.

Your data isn't sold or used for advertising.

Removing your data

Email the address shown on the Google sign-in screen for this site and ask to be removed. Your request and approval records will be deleted. You can also remove the site's access in your Google account settings under third-party connections.
